# Translate source material into Vietnamese

Use this skill when translating the Chinese source document or revising its Vietnamese translation.

## Workflow

1. Work only from the supplied source passage and its surrounding context. Preserve section order, meaning, qualifications, examples, and tone; do not silently omit or invent claims.
2. Write natural, accessible Vietnamese for readers in Vietnam. Prefer consistent terms and plain language over literal Chinese syntax.
3. This project publishes a Vietnam-localized adaptation, not a literal copy of China-specific guidance. Replace laws, regulations, public services, emergency numbers, government programs, currencies/prices, statistics, and other jurisdiction-dependent facts with current, relevant Vietnamese equivalents only after verification using `research-vn`. Never mechanically convert CNY to VND or swap a Chinese law/agency/phone number for a Vietnamese one. For topics where reliable Vietnam-specific evidence is scarce, international studies and statistics may be used if clearly identified with their country/region, population, year, source, and limitations; do not present them as data or recommendations specific to Vietnam. Do not use international evidence alone to assert Vietnamese legal requirements, public-service details, or locally applicable clinical guidance. If no reliable equivalent or current local fact can be verified, omit the unsupported claim or label it clearly as unavailable/pending research; do not preserve it as actionable Vietnamese guidance. Keep a brief source note explaining material localization.
4. Maintain a short glossary for recurring or ambiguous terms when working across multiple passages. Reuse established choices and flag unresolved alternatives.
5. Mark illegible, ambiguous, context-dependent, or potentially erroneous source text explicitly; request source context or human review instead of guessing.
6. Preserve headings, lists, tables, citations, and other useful structure in the output format requested. Do not fabricate citations or references.

## Output

Unless asked for another format, provide:
- The Vietnamese translation, preserving the source structure.
- A brief **Ghi chú biên tập** only where there are ambiguities, adaptation suggestions, or facts that require verification. Keep the translation distinct from those notes.
- Glossary updates only for new or changed recurring terms.

Do not claim that legal or factual statements have been verified by translation alone; use the `research-vn` skill when Vietnam-specific verification is needed.
